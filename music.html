Songs & Vocals Program

About Our Songs & Vocals Program

The Songs & Vocals program at IWACU FOUNDATION helps young artists develop their singing talent and musical expression. Participants learn voice control, stage confidence, songwriting, and performance skills while celebrating African culture through music.

Vocal Training Activities

Vocal Training

Learning voice control, pitch, and singing techniques.

Song Writing

Creating original songs and expressing creativity.

Stage Performance

Performing songs in concerts and community events.

Recording Sessions

Recording songs in studios and producing music.
