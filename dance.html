Traditional Dance Program

About Our Traditional Dance Program

IWACU FOUNDATION promotes African cultural heritage through traditional dance. Our dancers learn discipline, teamwork, and cultural pride while performing Rwandan traditional dances at community events, festivals, and celebrations.

Dance Activities

Traditional Drumming

Learning the powerful rhythms of African drums.

Cultural Dance

Performing Rwandan cultural dances and choreography.

Stage Performances

Participating in national and international events.

Cultural Exchange

Sharing Rwandan dance culture with the world.
