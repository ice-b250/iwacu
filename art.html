Visual Arts Program

About Our Visual Arts Program

The Visual Arts program at IWACU FOUNDATION empowers youth to express their creativity through painting, drawing, sculpture, and modern art. We encourage African artistic identity while teaching professional creative skills.

What We Teach

Painting

Learn modern and traditional painting techniques.

Drawing

Develop drawing skills and artistic imagination.

Sculpture

Create artistic sculptures using different materials.

Creative Design

Design posters, graphics and cultural art.
